# Research protocol outline

The repository can generate observational hypotheses. It is not a playback system and must not be used to expose animals to generated signals.

Before testing a functional claim:

1. State one preregistered hypothesis and one primary outcome.
2. Obtain species-specific ethics/welfare review, permits, consent, and a stopping rule.
3. Randomize stimuli and sessions; avoid pseudoreplication.
4. Match amplitude, duration, spectrum, novelty, and background conditions in controls.
5. Include sham/neutral conditions and negative controls.
6. Predefine response variables such as orientation, approach, latency, call-back, or affiliative behavior.
7. Blind behavioral scorers where possible.
8. Monitor fatigue, distress, social disruption, feeding/mating/parenting interference, and cumulative exposure.
9. Replicate across animals, sessions, and independent observers.
10. Report null results and deviations, not only successful responses.

An animal response to a playback may indicate discrimination, habituation, arousal, or novelty. It does not automatically establish a human-like proposition or semantics.
