# Research starting points

These are starting points for a literature review, not evidence that this repository performs translation.

- [Project CETI](https://www.projectceti.org/) — an example of the progression from synchronized sensing and modeling toward playback validation.
- [Google DeepMind: DolphinGemma](https://deepmind.google/models/gemma/dolphingemma/) — a structure/prediction-oriented animal vocalization model, not a general translation claim.
- [Earth Species Project](https://earthspecies.org/) — cross-species bioacoustic tooling and datasets.
- [Interactive playback and animal communication](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4528452/) — why signal function can depend on an exchange rather than an isolated sound.
- [Animal behavior research guidance from ASAB](https://www.asab.org/) — welfare and responsible research considerations.

For a real study, pin exact papers, dataset versions, licenses, and methods in the experiment record rather than relying on project summaries or product pages.
